# What do I do next?

A short daily checklist for field users.

## Start of day

- [ ] Check **Team Dashboard** or **Mission Control** for priorities.
- [ ] Review today's appointments.
- [ ] Follow up on open prospects.

## During the day

- [ ] Log new contacts in **Quick Capture**.
- [ ] Confirm interviews and update outcomes.
- [ ] Use approved scripts from **Scripts & Objection Handling**.

## End of day

- [ ] Clear follow-ups scheduled for tomorrow.
- [ ] Note any prospects needing upline support.

## Need help?

- Search this Knowledge Hub for scripts and procedures.
- Ask your upline for organization-specific policy questions.
