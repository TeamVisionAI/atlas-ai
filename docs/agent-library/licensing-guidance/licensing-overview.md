# Licensing overview (generic)

High-level guidance for recruiting conversations. **Confirm state-specific rules with your upline and compliance team.**

## Typical path (example)

1. **Study** — Complete pre-licensing education for your state.
2. **Exam** — Pass the state licensing exam.
3. **Background** — Complete background check and application steps required by your state.

## Talking points

- Licensing is required before someone can represent products.
- Timelines vary by person and state.
- Your organization may have a preferred study path — point prospects to official resources.

## Do not

- Guarantee exam results or licensing approval.
- Quote fees or timelines you have not verified.
