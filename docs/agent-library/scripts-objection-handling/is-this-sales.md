# Is this sales?

Use this as a starting script when a prospect asks whether the opportunity is sales.

## Acknowledge

- Thank them for asking directly.
- Confirm you are sharing information about a business opportunity, not pressuring them.

## Short answer (template)

> This is not a traditional retail job. It is a business opportunity where people build their own practice. Some people explore it part-time while keeping another job.

## One follow-up question

Ask one useful next question — for example whether they prefer to learn more by phone or in person.

## Do not

- Promise specific income.
- Claim guaranteed employment.
- Argue or dismiss their concern.
