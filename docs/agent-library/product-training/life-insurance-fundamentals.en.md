# Life insurance fundamentals (overview)

Educational talking points — not product recommendations.

## Core concepts

| Concept | Plain-language summary |
|--------|-------------------------|
| **Term** | Coverage for a set period; often used for temporary needs. |
| **Permanent** | Designed to last longer; may include cash value features depending on product. |

## Compliance-safe framing

- Explain concepts; do not recommend a specific product without proper fact-finding.
- Use organization-approved illustrations and disclosures.
- When unsure, escalate to a licensed upline.
