# Opportunity overview

A short talking-point outline for first conversations.

## What to cover

1. **Purpose** — You are inviting them to learn about a business opportunity (not a guaranteed job).
2. **Flexibility** — Some people explore part-time; others go full-time after licensing.
3. **Next step** — A brief conversation or interview to see if it is a fit.

## Sample opener

> I wanted to share an opportunity to build your own business with training and support. Would you be open to a short conversation this week?

## Keep it conversational

- One idea at a time.
- Pause for questions.
- Do not overload with licensing or product detail on the first message.
