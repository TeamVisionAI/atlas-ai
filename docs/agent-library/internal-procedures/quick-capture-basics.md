# Quick Capture basics

How field users add a new prospect in Atlas.

## When to use Quick Capture

- Met someone at an event or referral conversation.
- Need to add contact details before a full qualification flow.

## Steps

1. Open **Quick Capture** from the sidebar.
2. Enter name, phone, and source if available.
3. Save — Atlas creates the prospect in your workspace scope.
4. Follow up from **Mission Control** or **Prospect Center**.

## Tips

- Double-check phone number format.
- Add notes that help your future self (where you met, what they asked).
